My Hobbies and Interests

These are some of the things I enjoy doing in my free time.

01 / LEARN

Coding

I enjoy coding and exploring different programming languages. As a Computer Science student, I like learning how programs work, solving problems, and creating projects. I'm still improving my skills, but I enjoy the challenge of learning something new.

02 / LISTEN

Music

One of my favorite songs is “Fix You” by Coldplay . I love this song because of its emotional melody and meaningful message about hope, comfort, and healing. It reminds me that even when life gets difficult, things can get better. The way the song slowly builds up makes it even more powerful and beautiful to listen to. It's one of those songs that I can listen to over and over again without getting tired of it.

Games & Activities

I enjoy playing online and offline games such as Valorant, Minecraft, Roblox, Far Cry 3–6, Dishonored 1–2, and many more. Gaming is one of my favorite ways to relax and have fun. I also enjoy playing badminton, even though I'm not very good at it yet.
