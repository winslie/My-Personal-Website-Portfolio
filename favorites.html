Things close to my heart

My Favorites

These are some of the foods, songs, places, and other things that I enjoy and that mean a lot to me.

01

Favorite Foods

My favorite foods are lechon paksiw, sinigang, miswa, and Chickenjoy. These are some of the foods I grew up eating, which is why they have always been special to me. I enjoy their flavors, and I never really get tired of eating them. Some of these foods also remind me of home and the meals I shared with my family. Even if I get to try different dishes in the future, I think these will always be some of my favorites because they have been part of my life since I was young.

02

Favorite Music

Some of my favorite songs are “Fix You” by Coldplay, “How to Save a Life” by The Fray, and “Paalam” by Moira Dela Torre and Ben&Ben. I like these songs because of their beautiful melodies and the feelings they bring when I listen to them. Each song has its own message about life, love, struggles, and letting go. I especially enjoy songs that make me think about different experiences in life. Sometimes I listen to them because I can relate to their messages, and other times I simply enjoy the music. These are the kinds of songs that I can listen to again and again without getting tired of them.

03

Favorite Movies — Marvel

I really enjoy watching Marvel movies because I grew up watching them as a kid. I like the action scenes, the superheroes, the stories, and how different characters come together in some of the movies. Watching Marvel has always been fun for me, especially when there are exciting fights or unexpected moments. I'm not really a die-hard fan who knows every single character or every detail about the Marvel universe. I just enjoy watching the movies and having a good time. I'm a fan, but not the type who knows everything about it, I promise HAHAHA.

04

Favorite Colors

Red Black Pink

My favorite colors are red and black because I really like how they look, especially when they're combined. For me, these colors are simple but look really good together. I like how black gives a clean and modern look, while red makes things stand out more. I also started liking pink because it's my girlfriend's favorite color, hehe. Whenever I see something pink, it sometimes reminds me of her. So even though red and black are my main favorite colors, pink has also become a special color to me.

05

Favorite Places

My Home

Home will always be one of my favorite places because it's where I feel the most comfortable. It's where I can rest, relax, and be myself after a long and tiring day. No matter where I go, there's always something comforting about coming back home.

The Refiners Christian School (RCS) / Jesus Christ Refiners Fire (JCRF)

This place is like my second home because it's where I grew up and spent a big part of my life. It's not just a school and a church to me, but also a place filled with memories and people who are close to my heart. I've learned many things here, made friends, and shared many experiences. The people there have become an important part of my life, and I'm thankful to have grown up around them.

MOA Seaside

I also like MOA Seaside because it reminds me of my childhood. My parents used to take me there when I was younger, and I always enjoyed spending time with them. Whenever I think about this place, I remember those simple family trips and the happy moments we shared. It's a place that will always remind me of my family and the good times we had together.

06

Favorite Bible Verse — 2 Timothy 4:17

“But the Lord stood with me and gave me strength so that I might preach the Good News in its entirety for all the Gentiles...” — 2 Timothy 4:17 (NLT) · Read the full verse

Why I Like This Verse

This is one of my favorite Bible verses because it reminds me that God is always with me, especially during difficult times. There are moments when I feel tired, worried, or unsure about what to do, but this verse reminds me that I don't have to face everything on my own. God gives me strength when I need it and helps me continue even when things are hard. It also reminds me to trust Him and keep my faith no matter what happens in life. Knowing that God is always there gives me comfort and courage to face each day.

07

Favorite Quote

“To the world you may be one person, but to one person you are the world.” — Bill Wilson

Why I Like This Quote

I like this quote because it reminds me that we don't have to be important to everyone to make a difference in someone's life. Sometimes, even simple things like being there for someone, listening to them, or showing that we care can mean a lot. It reminds me to value the people who are close to me and appreciate the little things they do. We might just be ordinary people to others, but to our family, friends, or someone who loves us, we can mean so much more.
